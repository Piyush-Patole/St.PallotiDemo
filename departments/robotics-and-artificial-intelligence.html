About the Department

The Department of Robotics & Artificial Intelligence merges mechanical kinematics, sensor electronics, computer vision, and ROS-based autonomous navigation. Equipped with physical drone arenas, 6-axis robotic arms, and e-Yantra IIT Bombay setups, students engineer real-world robots for manufacturing, defense, and healthcare.
