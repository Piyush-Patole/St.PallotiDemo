About the Department

Established in 2004, the Department of Computer Science & Engineering has evolved into a premier center of computing education in central India. Accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA), the department provides state-of-the-art computing facilities, Gigabit fiber connectivity, and a curriculum tuned to global software demands.

V

Vision

To evolve as a center of excellence in computer science education and research, nurturing technically competent, socially conscious, and ethical IT professionals.

M

Mission

To provide quality education through progressive curricula and experiential learning, fostering industry partnerships, entrepreneurial spirit, and research innovation.
